Struktured Facts makes music the way you build anything meant to last — from the ground up, with nothing left to chance. His verses are plain-spoken and exact: what happened, what it meant, and what he did about it.

Signed to Purpose Wave Entertainment, he treats every song as a level of the same structure. His debut, Honest Observations, arrived in July 2026 — sixteen tracks of seeing clear and speaking truth. TIERS followed the same year: twenty tracks about the climb, told one step at a time.

"You can argue with opinions. You can't argue with facts."
